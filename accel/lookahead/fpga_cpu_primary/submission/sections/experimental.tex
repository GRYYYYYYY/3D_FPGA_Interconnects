\section{Experimental Method and Evidence}
\subsection{Frozen Inputs, Cohorts and Resources}
We reuse completed experiments; paper integration adds no solver runs. The STRICT corpus contains 603 formal records: 414 legal full-flow and 189 construction-only observations. Capped route tails do not establish legal routes. Failed large-input qualifications remain in attempted denominators. GEMM and core-scaling cohorts are separate.

Native ALT cohort R6.1 measures BNN and s38417 with Stock, Flat, ALT CPU and original ALT CUDA. R6.2 preselects LSTM seed2 before ALT tuning and measures those arms plus Pipeline on another worker. Its 43 managed runs comprise 33 formal timings, nine qualifications and one profile. Old-case Pipeline supplements and later conversion pairs are separate phases, never paired post hoc.

LSTM is a same-family tuning holdout, previously present in the CPU width120 study. It shares BNN's architecture SHA but has different netlist, placement, RRG and actual search inputs. LSTM has 4,166,724 nodes and 40,142,314 edges; BNN has 4,102,608 nodes and 39,480,616 edges. Both use two layers, width120 and 40 samples, not independent architectures.

The baseline is VTR commit \texttt{0b912334fdd8}, release build with assertion level 2. Native ALT uses a Xeon Gold 6530 with eight distinct physical cores in one NUMA node and one H100 80GB, executing solvers sequentially. Shared instances are not exclusive. R6.2 rotates arm order across three processes. The artifact binds inputs, commands, hosts and binaries; hosts and phases are not pooled.

\subsection{Timing and Statistical Policy}
E1 is sample service; the CUDA wrapper ends E1 before final ABI output copy. E2 is native \texttt{MapLookahead::compute}: conversion, preparation/service, output handoff, group merge, interpolation, source/OPIN and derived minima. Process adds RR XML loading, initialization, routing, checking/STA and uniform serialization. Independent route readback is outside Process; pre-generated RRG, packing and placement are excluded. Caches are not flushed.

For paired repeat $r$, $s_r=T_{c,r}/T_{a,r}$. We report mean and sample SD of these ratios and of raw seconds ($n=3$). SD is variability, not a confidence interval; repeats are processes, not designs or seeds. Stock E2 has 0.01-second log precision; other E2 timers are high resolution. Diagnostic capture, complete-pair verification and profiling are outside formal timings.

\subsection{Qualification and Attribution}
STRICT checks native wire-map bytes under its proposition. ALT separately checks complete CPU/CUDA sample pairs and maps, then each candidate route through independent original-VPR FULL/STA readback with unchanged route SHA, zero overuse, and CPD/wirelength within $+5\%$ of control. LSTM uses both Flat and Stock controls.

A read-only audit explains an isolated outer-boundary CHANZ diagnostic subset from coordinate rules and degrees; it neither repairs graphs nor validates all RRG invariants. Two retained CPU bounding-box notification repairs can change routing trajectories despite identical wire maps. The unique complete cause is not established. Stock ratios therefore describe the implementation combination; shared-repair Flat/Pipeline/ALT comparisons better isolate construction.

Table~\ref{tab:physical} reports the complete connected core, excluding the board shell, PCIe and memory controller. The final RTL uses 93.59\% of device LUTs after standard optimization: 932,268 logic LUTs, 286,776 LUTRAM and 1,024 SRLs. Optimization removes 3,202 LUTs and 674 FFs. Placement uses 1,212,828 LUTs and 749,091 FFs, but occupies 99.86\% of CLB sites. All 64 banks, 16 frontends, 32 ports and 9,681 nonconstant output bits remain; there are no black boxes. Source closures, global and hierarchical reports and checkpoint hashes bind these counts.

The preceding configuration fails global routing under both default and SSI-spread placement, with approximately 5.8 million trial-routing overlaps. The SSI failure checkpoint has setup WNS/TNS of $-15.487/-3{,}098{,}668.250$ ns and hold WHS/THS of $-3.530/-1{,}410{,}972.500$ ns. Its worst reported setup path crosses three SLR boundaries from bank completion payload to dispatcher error state; estimated net delay contributes 17.500 of 19.854 ns. Placed CLB-site occupancy is 99.92\%, despite lower aggregate LUT utilization. These are failure diagnostics, not routed timing. They motivated the single return-payload word-multiplexer revision rather than increasing parallelism.

The final RTL passes synthesis and standard optimization; SSI placement completes, but no completed, qualified full-core route is established in this evidence snapshot. The 5 ns constraint is a target, not a measured period. All 1,237,261 post-opt sequential clock pins connect to the core clock; the reset BUFG has zero clock loads. Placement-inserted buffers require the same connectivity classification. No reset/enable buffer is silently counted as a core-clock source. Zero reported DRC/check-timing errors do not resolve unplaced timing, the retained out-of-context reset warning, methodology warnings, or 138 unproven generated constant aliases.

Consequently, qualified full-core frequency, FPGA execution time and measured CPU/FPGA speedup are \emph{not established}. The assumed-frequency model is a conditional architecture study. Neither local-module timing, synthesis completion nor an unsuccessful-route WNS is converted into an achieved FPGA result.

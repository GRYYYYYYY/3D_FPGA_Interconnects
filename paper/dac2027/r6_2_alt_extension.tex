\paragraph{Frozen holdout and scope.} LSTM seed2 was selected before timing independently of shift/cache tuning: two layers, width120, 4,166,724 final RR nodes, 40,142,314 edges and 40 samples, within unchanged $N<2^{23}$ admission. E2 is the whole native lookahead constructor; Process includes RR XML loading, initialization, routing, checks and STA. Prior input generation is excluded; filesystem cache is not flushed.

\paragraph{Qualification.} Live CUDA compares every sample pair with a same-input CPU reference in a diagnostic run. Formal runs exclude capture and that verification. The no-fanin audit explains isolated outer-boundary CHANZ using exact channel-coordinate rules and final degrees; it does not certify all RRG invariants. No graph or checker was changed. Stock E2 has 0.01-second log precision; other E2 values use the high-resolution constructor timer. Timer formatting is not measurement accuracy.

The earlier report used ratios of medians, $3.348\times$ and $1.095\times$. These statistics are not interchangeable. R6.2 Pipeline supplements for s38417 and BNN pass map/QoR checks on a new worker; they do not support ratios to old-worker CUDA times. LSTM supplies the new same-worker Pipeline comparison.

\paragraph{System limit.} In BNN R6.1 repeat1, E1 is 0.363794840\,s of E2=3.104359912\,s (11.72\%). Keeping every other interval fixed, eliminating E1 gives at most 1.132744$\times$ E2 or 1.005434$\times$ Process speedup. This does not constrain conversion/preparation optimization. Stage partitions use one process; child medians are never summed. Historical E1/service ends before final ABI output handoff; E2 includes it.

\paragraph{Hardware stop.} Read-only Vivado 2022.2 identifies 960 URAM, 2016 BRAM36 and 3 SLRs in U280. XRT finds no FPGA and platforminfo no installed platform. One 32-bit member per 4096$\times$72 URAM word needs 1024 URAM for 16\,MiB, exceeding the raw device; two members per word need 512 but do not establish ports or placement. Cache lookup/LRU mapping and random dependent RMW service remain unproved. Software hit counts are not hardware throughput. Proposal traffic alone implies 118.00\,GB/s break-even and 147.50\,GB/s with 20\% time margin against the frozen warm compact-CUDA E1 target; other traffic is omitted. No achieved frequency or calibrated whole-pair HBM service is available. Status is UNRESOLVED\_NO\_SUPPORTED\_MEMORY\_EVIDENCE; FPGA superiority is false. No HLS/RTL/OOC or new replay was launched.

\paragraph{Evidence scope.} Three runtime repeats of one holdout placement do not establish all-architecture or all-size generality. The strict CPU paper and this ALT appendix remain separate. Per-case/backend/shift/representation records, negative outcomes, source/object bindings and raw logs are archived in the R6.2 evidence snapshot. No automatic next hardware round follows.

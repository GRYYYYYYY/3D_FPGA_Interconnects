Native multicore routing leaves costly lookahead construction insufficiently parallelized. We present a semantics-preserving decomposition for 3D FPGA lookahead: independent layer-aware floods, ordered cost reduction, and cross-group execution with reusable private workspaces. Slice-local interpolation and compact storage further expose scheduling and memory tradeoffs. On eight-core GEMM, construction falls from 149.56 to 20.95 seconds and process speedup is 1.638 ± 0.005×. On two large 3D graphs, Pipeline scales approximately 12× from one to sixteen cores while group-local execution saturates. Native equivalence checks, full-routing experiments, and direct global-task controls establish the benefit of removing group barriers and a verified task boundary for future hardware acceleration.

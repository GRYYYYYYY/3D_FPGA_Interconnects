                   VI. S COPE AND L IMITATIONS

   STRICT equality remains conditional; ALT route qualiﬁca-
tion covers tested graphs, not a universal QoR bound. Three
process repetitions, shared hosts and same-family architectures
limit generalization. Controls and ALT retain RRG no-fanin
diagnostics: FULL/STA validates actual routes, not every
graph-generation choice. Dense state, scans and launch costs
bound GPU applicability. The evidence supports complemen-
tary mechanisms, not independent multiplicative C1/C2 effects
or an automatic backend selector.

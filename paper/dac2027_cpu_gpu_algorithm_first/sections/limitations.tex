\section{Scope and Limitations}
STRICT equality requires the stated sample, visitation, reduction and numeric conditions; it does not imply identical route trajectories. ALT qualification covers tested routes, not arbitrary graphs or a universal QoR bound. LSTM is a same-family tuning holdout, not an independent architecture. Three process repetitions and shared instances limit small-difference claims. The GPU implementation retains dense state and scans, making capacity and launch costs part of its applicability. No automatic backend selector is established. Construction-only controls, native eight-core runs and packed resident measurements have different boundaries and are not pooled. The evidence supports complementary mechanisms and concrete execution configurations, not independent multiplicative C1/C2 effects.

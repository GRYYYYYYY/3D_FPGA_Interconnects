     VI. LIMITATIONS AND HARDWARE BOUNDARY

  The STRICT proposition covers wire-map semantics under
explicit assumptions. ALT provides measured CPU/CUDA
agreement and qualiﬁed downstream behavior on the tested
inputs, with congestion counterexamples to native equivalence.
Wider seed, architecture and numerical-domain claims would
require new evidence. LSTM is an ALT-tuning holdout from
the same architecture family and previously appeared in the
CPU study. Shared instances, small repetition counts and un-
controlled ﬁlesystem caches limit interpretation of variability
and deployment performance.
  FPGA superiority remains unveriﬁed: there is no board mea-
surement, achieved implementation frequency or calibrated
dependent random whole-pair RMW service. Logical stor-
age totals and software cache hits do not establish physical

C. Actual Full-Core Physical Status
   The complete connected XCU280 core excludes the board
shell, PCIe and memory controller. Standard optimization re-
moves 3,202 LUTs and 674 FFs. Post-opt LUT use is 93.59%:
932,268 logic LUTs, 286,776 LUTRAM and 1,024 SRLs.

TRAM, 896 SRL), but occupies 99.86% of CLB sites. All 64
banks, 16 frontends, 32 ports and 9,681 nonconstant output
bits remain, with no black boxes.

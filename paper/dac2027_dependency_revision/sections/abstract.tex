Layer-aware lookahead construction retains source-relative cost semantics but need not retain every execution barrier. We decouple dependencies at two granularities: STRICT schedules private samples across groups with native-order reduction; ALT separately defines whole-pair FP32 waves within searches. Matched scheduling and wave-work controls identify the exposed concurrency. On a native LSTM same-family tuning holdout, CUDA achieves a $3.207\times$ construction speedup and a $1.094\times$ complete-process speedup over the eight-thread CPU Pipeline baseline. Small-input slowdowns and representative-cost differences delimit these benefits. A finite FPGA mapping supplies RTL/model validation and conditional memory-service predictions; qualified full-core frequency and FPGA superiority remain unestablished.

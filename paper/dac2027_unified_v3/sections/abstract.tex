A change to a 3D FPGA routing-resource graph or its indexed costs can require a fresh lookahead table before routing. Each sampled search contributes a source-relative delay and congestion pair to this table. We identify two opportunities for parallel execution. STRICT issues private native searches across group boundaries and consumes their results in their original order. Under explicit conditions on recording, ties and interpolation, it reproduces the native wire table. ALT changes the search rule to process complete FP32 labels in bucket waves, with CPU and CUDA implementations of the same rule. A profile on 40 BNN samples shows how wider waves trade additional edge work for fewer synchronization rounds. GEMM and BNN each complete six configurations with three repeats on one eight-core/H100 worker. Paired Stock/ALT CUDA Process speedups are 1.80 and 1.88 on GEMM and BNN, respectively. The corresponding construction speedups are 20.03 and 19.97. Results apply to fixed-input routing on these two-layer graphs. Auxiliary LSTM checks confirm STRICT map identity and ALT backend agreement.

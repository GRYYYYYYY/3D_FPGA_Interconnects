A change to a 3D FPGA routing-resource graph or its indexed costs can require a fresh lookahead table before routing. Each sampled search contributes a source-relative delay and congestion pair to this table. We identify two opportunities for parallel execution. STRICT issues private native searches across group boundaries and consumes their results in their original order. Under explicit conditions on recording, ties and interpolation, it reproduces the native wire table. ALT changes the search rule to process complete FP32 labels in bucket waves, with CPU and CUDA implementations of the same rule. A profile on 40 BNN samples shows how wider waves trade additional edge work for fewer synchronization rounds. We specify a single-worker native evaluation on three complete two-layer graphs and distinguish construction from complete fixed-input routing time. Unified runtime and route-quality measurements are pending.
